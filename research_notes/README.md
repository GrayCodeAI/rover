# Research notes (historical)

These notes are dated research snapshots, not descriptions of current Rover
behavior. The `Rover OSS comparison 2026/` notes were committed on 2026-09-24 and
feed the summary in `reports/Rover OSS comparison 2026.md`.

- Citations such as `file:line` and relative links point at the tree as it was on
  the research date. Many lines have moved since.
- The Python, TypeScript, and Go SDK source trees were removed after the research
  (see `CHANGELOG.md`). `sdk/...` links, `rover_client` references, and
  `make sdk-test` results describe code and a make target that no longer exist.
- Third-party project facts (stars, features, licenses) were true when retrieved and
  may have changed.

For current status read `STATUS.md`; for the current plan read
`docs/design/RUST_PARITY_PLAN.md`.
