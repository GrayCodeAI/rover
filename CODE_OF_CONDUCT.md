# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Technical disagreement should address evidence and code,
not personal attributes. Harassment, threats, doxxing, credential exposure, discrimination,
and retaliation are not acceptable. Keep reports and logs free of unrelated private data.

Maintainers of the eventual hosted project may remove harmful content and restrict access
through a documented, proportionate process. A private contact and escalation procedure
must be established before opening a public contributor community; none is invented here.
